# Ingredients

* 4 boneless pork chops
* 1 T. olive oil
* 1 C. parmesan cheese (I used Kraft)
* 1 C. Italian bread crumbs
* 1 tsp. pepper
* 1 tsp. garlic powder


# Directions
1. On a plate combine the last 4 ingredients. 
2. Rub the pork chops with olive oil and then dip (coat) each one in the cheese mixture. 
3. Press the mixture over the pork chops to make sure they are well covered in it. 
4. Line a pan with tin foil and spray with cooking spray. 
5. Place the pork chops on the pan and bake at 350 degrees for 40-45 minutes.
