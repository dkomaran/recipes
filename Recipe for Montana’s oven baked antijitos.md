**Montana’s Oven-Baked Antojitos** are a staple on Canadian pub menus: warm, crispy flour tortillas rolled around a rich, tangy cream cheese filling packed with jalapeños, bell peppers, and melted cheddar.  
They are traditionally served with Montana's signature **pesto sour cream** for dipping.

### **Prep & Cook Info**

> * **Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
> * **Bake Time:** 12–15 minutes  
> * **Servings:** Makes 4–5 large rolled wraps (approx. 24–30 pinwheels)

### **Ingredients**

#### **For the Antojitos:**

> * **4–5 large** flour tortillas (10-inch burrito size)  
> * **8 oz (225g)** brick cream cheese, room temperature  
> * **2 tbsp** sour cream (softens the filling)  
> * **1 cup** sharp cheddar or Tex-Mex blend cheese, shredded  
> * **1–2** jalapeño peppers, seeded and very finely diced  
> * **½** red bell pepper, very finely diced  
> * **2** green onions (scallions), thinly sliced  
> * **½ tsp** garlic powder  
> * **1 tsp** Tex-Mex or Cajun seasoning (or a dash of hot sauce)  
> * **Optional:** 1 tbsp melted butter or olive oil for brushing

#### **For the Montana’s Dipping Sauce:**

> * **½ cup** sour cream  
> * **1–2 tbsp** basil pesto (or garlic pesto)  
> * *Salsa on the side (optional)*

### **Instructions**

> 1. **Preheat & Prep:** Preheat your oven to **375°F (190°C)**. Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.  
> 2. **Make the Filling:** In a medium mixing bowl, beat the softened cream cheese and 2 tbsp sour cream together until smooth. Stir in the garlic powder, Tex-Mex seasoning, diced jalapeños, red bell pepper, green onions, and shredded cheddar.  
> 3. **Fill & Roll the Tortillas:**  
   * Lay out a tortilla and spread about ¼ cup of the cream cheese mixture evenly over the surface, leaving a **1-inch border** clear around the outer edge (this prevents filling from squeezing out when rolling).  
   * Roll the tortilla up **tightly** like a log or cigar.  
> 4. **Score the Wraps:** Place the rolled tortillas seam-side down on your prepared baking sheet. Using a serrated knife, **score diagonal cuts about ¾ of the way through** the roll every inch (or slice them completely into individual pinwheels before baking if you prefer crispier edges). *(Optional: Lightly brush the tops with melted butter or oil for extra crunch and color).*  
> 5. **Bake:** Bake for **12 to 15 minutes** until the cheese inside is hot and melted, and the tortilla edges are golden brown and crisp. If desired, broil for the last 1–2 minutes to get a bit of extra browning.  
> 6. **Make the Dip & Serve:** Whisk together the sour cream and basil pesto in a small bowl until combined. Finish slicing the antojitos all the way through into individual pinwheel pieces, and serve warm with the dip\!

**Pro Tip:** Finely dice the bell peppers and jalapeños, then pat them dry with a paper towel before stirring them into the cream cheese. Excess moisture from fresh veggies can make the filling watery inside the wrap.
